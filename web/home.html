Welcome to HISCA

HISCA is a computational platform for predicting associations between non-coding RNAs and drugs. It integrates RNA sequence representations, multiple views of drug molecules, and heterogeneous network information to score ncRNA–drug pairs.

The server supports miRNAs, lncRNAs, and circRNAs. In addition to association scores, it provides region–substructure interaction maps for exploring the model's learned interaction patterns.

Choose Predict & explain to analyze an individual pair, or Drug → top ncRNAs to rank candidate ncRNAs for a selected drug.
